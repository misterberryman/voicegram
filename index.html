How to use

🎤 Microphone: Tap Start to enable the mic and see the live spectrogram.

👆 Crosshair: Move your finger or mouse over the spectrogram for frequency, note and loudness.

💥 Click to Hear: Tap anywhere to play a 2-second sine wave at that frequency.

🎙️ Recording: Tap Record to capture audio, then replay it.

🎼 Voice Ranges: Overlay a typical range for Soprano/Alto/Tenor/Baritone/Bass.

🎯 Target Pitch: Draw a green target line; your pitch turns green within ±3%.

📈 Harmonics: Toggle ON for a bar chart and table of up to 16 overtones.

🔍 Debug: Leave this panel open until Start works, then hide it.
