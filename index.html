How to use

🎤 Microphone: Tap Start to enable mic and see real-time spectrogram. Speak or sing to visualize harmonics.

👆 Crosshair: Move your finger/mouse over the spectrogram. Hover shows frequency, note name, and loudness.

💥 Click to Hear: Tap anywhere on the spectrogram to play a 2-second sine wave at that frequency.

🎙️ Recording: Tap Record to capture audio. Replay and compare later.

🎼 Voice Ranges: Select Soprano/Alto/Tenor/Baritone/Bass to see typical ranges overlayed.

🎯 Target Pitch: Choose a note to draw a green target line. Your pitch turns green when within ±3%.

📈 Harmonics: Toggle ON to see strength of up to 16 overtones in a bar chart and table. Helps analyze vocal richness, breathiness, and resonance.

💾 Export: Save spectrogram images, download recordings, or export session logs for lesson notes.
